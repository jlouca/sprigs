Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Sprigs ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where applicable, Quebec's Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector (Law 25 / Bill 64).

By creating an account you consent to the collection, use, and storage of your personal information as described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

Sprigs is operated by TheDirtyGardener, located in Quinte West, Ontario, Canada.

Privacy Officer contact: thedirtygardener101@gmail.com
Mailing address: 57 Crown St, Trenton, Ontario K0K 1K0

2. What Personal Information We Collect

  • Email address — collected at registration and used solely for authentication and transactional emails (e.g. email verification, password reset).
  • Plant data — plant names, categories, dates, and notes you create within the app.
  • Photos — images you upload for plants and notes, stored in cloud storage.

We collect only the information necessary to operate the service. We do not collect payment information, location data, or any information about minors.

3. Why We Collect It (Purposes)

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To store and display your plant records across devices.
  • To send transactional emails required by the service (verification, password reset).
  • To respond to support or privacy inquiries.

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored (Cross-Border Transfer)

Sprigs uses Supabase as its backend provider. Your data is stored on servers located in the United States (AWS us-east-1). This means your personal information is transferred outside of Canada.

Under PIPEDA, we are responsible for personal information transferred to a third party for processing, and we have ensured Supabase provides a comparable level of protection.

Under Quebec Law 25, we disclose that this cross-border transfer occurs and have documented this in our Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA). If you are a Quebec resident and have questions about this transfer, please contact our Privacy Officer.

5. Consent

By creating an account you provide express consent to the collection, use, and storage of your personal information as described in this policy.

You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see Section 8). Withdrawal of consent will result in the immediate and permanent deletion of all your personal information from our systems.

6. Retention

Your personal information is retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all data (email, plant records, notes, and photos) is permanently and immediately deleted from our database and cloud storage. Supabase infrastructure backups may retain data for up to 7 days following deletion before being purged.

7. Security

We implement the following safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text (managed by Supabase Auth with bcrypt hashing).
  • Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensure each user can only access their own data.
  • Access to the database is restricted by Supabase's role-based access controls.

In the event of a data breach that poses a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected users and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada within the timeframes required by PIPEDA. Quebec residents will additionally be notified in accordance with Law 25.

8. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Erasure (Right to be Forgotten) — delete your account directly within the app, which immediately removes all your personal information.
  • Portability (Quebec Law 25) — request your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the service and delete your account at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint — file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, for Quebec residents, the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI).

To exercise any of these rights, use the "Delete account" option within the app, or contact our Privacy Officer at thedirtygardener101@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days as required by law.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

Sprigs uses your browser's localStorage to store your authentication session token. This is strictly necessary for the app to function and does not track you across websites. No third-party cookies are set by Sprigs. Closing your browser or logging out removes the session.

10. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — database, authentication, and file storage. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr CDN — delivers the Lucide icon library and Supabase JS SDK.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — delivers styling. No data is collected by this CDN.

11. Children's Privacy

Sprigs is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact our Privacy Officer for immediate removal.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. If changes materially affect how we use your personal information, we will seek fresh consent.
